Pretzel Party Support

How to play

Every player keeps one finger in their own circle. The circles drift, orbit and swap places, and the round ends the moment anyone lets go. Your score is how many seconds the group survived, and the best time is saved for each player count.

Restoring Pro

Bought Pro on another device or reinstalled the app? On the main menu tap Remove ads, then Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
