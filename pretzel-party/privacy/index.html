Pretzel Party Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the Pretzel Party app ("the app") handles. The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your settings (such as player count and circle size), your best times, and whether you have purchased Pro. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you decline, the IDFA is not shared.
  • Device and app information, IP address (used for approximate location), ad interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics.

Google uses this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. Users in applicable US states can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information through the privacy options in the app. You can change tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Buying Pro removes all ads, and after that no ad requests are made.

Purchases

Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your payment details. Purchases are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

We do not run our own servers for the app, do not collect personal information ourselves, and do not sell your data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
